Trilingual Marketplace

An online marketplace that connects Afghan sellers with buyers at home and abroad, in three languages.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Trilingual Marketplace

The challenge

Small sellers had no simple way to reach buyers outside their city, and buyers needed a safe way to pay. Many customers also prefer to pay in cash or to agree on a price first.

What we built

We built a marketplace with a seller portal, product listings, a bargaining system where buyers can make offers, and checkout with cash on delivery or escrow for online payments.

How it works

  • FastAPI backendA fast Python API handles products, orders, offers and payments.
  • PostgreSQL databaseAll products, sellers and orders are stored safely in a relational database.
  • React front endA quick, modern interface that works well on phones and switches between three languages.
  • Escrow flowFor online payments, the money is held until the buyer confirms the order, then released to the seller.

Key features

  • Seller portalSellers add products, manage stock and follow their orders.
  • Make an offerBuyers bargain; sellers accept, decline or counter.
  • Escrow paymentsMoney is held safely until the buyer confirms delivery.
  • Cash on deliveryFor customers who prefer to pay when the order arrives.
  • Three languagesThe whole store works in Dari, Pashto and English.
  • Order trackingClear status for every order, from packing to delivery.
  • Admin dashboardApprove sellers, manage categories and watch sales.
  • Mobile-firstFast on phones and slow connections.
