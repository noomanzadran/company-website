Shams School ERP

One system for the whole school: students, attendance, exams and results, fees, HR and payroll. It works in three languages and keeps running without internet.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Shams School ERP

The challenge

Many schools still run on paper registers and spreadsheets. Fees are hard to track, results take days to prepare in the Ministry format, and most software on the market does not support Dari, Pashto or Solar Hijri dates. The internet is also not reliable enough for a system that the whole school depends on every day.

What we built

We built a complete school ERP with separate panels for the owner, admin, HR, finance, teachers and students. Each role sees only what it needs, from daily attendance to the monthly fee report.

How it works

  • Runs on the school networkThe system is installed on a local server inside the school, so teachers and staff use it in the browser on any computer, even when the internet is down.
  • Built with PHP and MySQLA proven, light stack that runs well on ordinary school computers and is easy to maintain.
  • Three languages, right-to-leftEvery screen works in Dari, Pashto and English, with right-to-left layouts and Solar Hijri dates.
  • Safe updatesNew versions are applied with database migrations, and the system takes an automatic backup first, so no data is lost.

Key features

  • Students & classesAdmissions, profiles, classes, sections and transfers in one place.
  • Daily attendanceTeachers mark attendance in seconds; parents can be informed of absences.
  • Exams & resultsMarks entry, automatic totals and result sheets in the Ministry format.
  • Fees & receiptsFees in AFN with discounts, balances and printed A4 receipts.
  • HR & payrollStaff records, attendance, leave and monthly salary sheets.
  • ReportsReady reports for the owner, finance and management, printable or exportable.
  • Three languagesDari, Pashto and English with Solar Hijri dates.
  • Safe by designRole-based access, light and dark mode, automatic backups.
