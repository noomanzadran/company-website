About Us

Technology built in Afghanistan, for Afghanistan.

Shams Technology Team is a Kabul-based team of eight engineers, developers and specialists. We build software that fits the way people and organisations here really work: in Dari, Pashto and English, mobile-first for low-bandwidth connections, and reliable on the devices people actually use.

Our work ranges from AI chatbots serving thousands of students to e-commerce marketplaces, national education platforms and management systems. We take each project from idea to launch, and we stay with our clients after it goes live.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, send us a message and we will reply.

1. What kind of projects do you take on?

Websites, school and business systems (ERP), AI chatbots and automation. We take a project from the first idea to launch, and we keep supporting it after that.

2. Do your systems support Dari and Pashto?

Yes. Our systems work in Dari, Pashto and English, with right-to-left layouts and Solar Hijri dates where they are needed.

3. Can the system keep working when the internet is down?

Yes. Our school ERP runs on the local network and keeps working offline, with automatic backups so no data is lost.

4. How long does a project take?

A simple website usually takes a few weeks. Larger systems are built in phases, so you see working parts early and can give feedback as we go.

5. Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer hosting, updates, backups and monitoring, and we fix problems quickly when something needs attention.

6. Can you add AI to a system we already use?

Yes. We can add a chatbot or a data assistant that answers questions from your own data, in your users’ languages, on the web, WhatsApp or Telegram.
