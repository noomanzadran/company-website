Student Messaging & Automation

WhatsApp broadcasts to thousands of students by grade and language, plus an automated registration pipeline.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Student Messaging & Automation

The challenge

Important messages had to reach thousands of students quickly, in the right language, and registrations were entered by hand.

What we built

We built a messaging system that sends WhatsApp broadcasts to selected groups, and an automated pipeline that handles registrations from form to confirmation.

How it works

  • WhatsApp Business APISends approved message templates to many students at once.
  • Audience filtersMessages go only to the chosen grades and languages.
  • Power Automate pipelineA new registration is saved, confirmed and reported without manual work.
  • AWSHosts the services that run the broadcasts and the automation.

Key features

  • Targeted broadcastsSend to chosen grades, classes and languages only.
  • Message templatesApproved templates in Dari, Pashto and English.
  • Delivery trackingSee sent, delivered and read for every broadcast.
  • Automatic confirmationsEvery new registration gets a WhatsApp confirmation.
  • Registration pipelineForm, sheet, confirmation and report, with no manual work.
  • Duplicate checkThe same student is not registered twice.
  • Scheduled messagesPrepare messages now and send them at the right time.
  • Weekly reportsStaff receive a summary by email every Friday.

We can also add

Options we can build on top of this system for a new client.

  • Two-way chatbot for common questions
  • Fee reminders and payment links
  • Parent groups
  • Attendance alerts
  • Integration with the school ERP
