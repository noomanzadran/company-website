University AI Assistant

An AI assistant that answers students’ questions about courses, fees, registration and exams, at any time of day.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. University AI Assistant

The challenge

The student office received the same questions again and again, by phone and in person, especially during registration and exam periods.

What we built

We built an AI assistant that answers from the university’s own documents and information, in the student’s language, on the website, WhatsApp and Telegram.

How it works

  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)The university’s documents are split into small pieces and indexed. For each question, the assistant first finds the right pieces, then writes the answer from them, so it stays accurate.
  • Gemini language modelWrites clear, natural answers in Dari, Pashto and English.
  • One brain, three channelsThe same assistant serves the website chat, WhatsApp and Telegram.
  • Docker deploymentEverything runs in containers, so it is easy to update and move to another server.

Key features

  • Answers from real documentsEvery answer comes from the university’s own files, with the source shown.
  • Three languagesStudents ask in Dari, Pashto or English and get answers in the same language.
  • Web, WhatsApp, TelegramOne assistant, available where students already are.
  • Knowledge baseStaff upload PDF, Word or Excel files; the assistant learns them automatically.
  • Quick buttonsCommon topics like fees, exam dates and courses are one tap away.
  • AnalyticsSee the top questions, busy times and which channels students use.
  • Hand-over to staffQuestions the assistant cannot answer can be passed to the student office.
  • Always available24/7 answers, even during registration rush.

We can also add

Options we can build on top of this system for a new client.

  • Voice messages in local languages
  • Student login for personal results and fees
  • Integration with the student information system
  • Announcements sent to all students
  • Admin chat for staff questions
