National Education Platform

A nationwide learning platform for students and teachers. We built the infrastructure and manage all of its educational content.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. National Education Platform

The challenge

The platform had to serve students and teachers across the country, with many video lessons, and stay available under heavy use.

What we built

We set up the platform and its hosting infrastructure, and we manage all of the educational content: video lessons, materials and course structure.

How it works

  • Learning platformLessons are organised by grade and subject, with videos, worksheets and practice questions.
  • InfrastructureServers and hosting are set up for many users at the same time, with monitoring and backups.
  • Content managementOur team uploads, organises and updates the lessons.

Key features

  • Video lessonsLessons by grade and subject, with a clear player and playlist.
  • Worksheets and quizzesEvery lesson can include downloadable material and a quiz.
  • Admin panelUpload, review and publish lessons in a simple workflow.
  • Built for scaleHosting and infrastructure ready for many students at once.
  • Monitoring and backupsThe platform is watched and backed up continuously.
  • Usage reportsViews, active students and hours watched.
  • Content managementOur team keeps the lessons organised and up to date.
  • Works on phonesStudents learn from any device.

We can also add

Options we can build on top of this system for a new client.

  • Download lessons for offline viewing
  • Live classes
  • Certificates for completed courses
  • Teacher training courses
  • Lesson subtitles in Dari and Pashto
