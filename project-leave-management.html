Leave Management System

An online leave system that replaced paper forms for more than 90 employees.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Leave Management System

The challenge

Leave requests were made on paper. Forms got lost, approvals were slow, and nobody could see leave balances easily.

What we built

We built an online system where employees request leave, managers approve it in two steps, and everyone sees their balance.

How it works

  • Role-based dashboardsEmployees, managers and HR each see their own dashboard.
  • Two-step approvalA request goes to the line manager first, then to HR.
  • Email alertsEach step sends an email automatically, so nothing waits on someone’s desk.
  • PHP and MySQLA simple, reliable stack that the organisation can host itself.

Key features

  • Online requestsEmployees request leave in a few clicks, from any device.
  • Eight leave typesIncluding annual, sick, emergency and unpaid leave.
  • Two-step approvalLine manager first, then HR, with a clear status at every step.
  • Leave balancesEveryone sees how many days they have left.
  • Email alertsEach step notifies the next person automatically.
  • Team calendarManagers see who is away on any day.
  • Role-based dashboardsDifferent views for employees, managers and HR.
  • ReportsLeave history and summaries for HR.

We can also add

Options we can build on top of this system for a new client.

  • Attendance and timesheets
  • Mobile app with push notifications
  • Payroll integration
  • Public holiday calendar per office
  • Single sign-on with company accounts
