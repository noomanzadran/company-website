Questions from schools

If your question is not answered here, contact us and we will reply.

1. How do we get started?

We show you a demo, agree on the modules you need, install the system, add your school's information and train your staff.

2. Can we bring our existing data from Excel?

Yes. Students and other records can be imported in bulk, so you do not have to type them again.

3. Do you train our staff?

Yes. We train administrators, teachers and accountants on the parts of the system they use.

4. Which languages does it support?

Dari, Pashto and English, with right-to-left screens and Solar Hijri dates. Each user can choose their language.

5. Does it work without internet?

Yes. The local version runs on a server inside your school and keeps working when the internet is down. Schools with several branches can use the cloud version.

6. Can it be adapted to our school?

Yes. Fees, exam rules, reports, user roles and the modules you use can be adjusted to your school.

7. What support do you provide?

We provide updates, backups and help by phone, WhatsApp or in person, and we fix problems quickly.

8. How much does it cost?

The price depends on the number of students, the modules you choose and whether you use the local or cloud version. Contact us for a quote.
