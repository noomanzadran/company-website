Modules in detail

What each part of the system does for your school

Students and admissions

A complete digital file for every student, from application to graduation.

  • Add students with photo; each student gets a Student ID and a first password
  • Find students by class, section and year, and export them to Excel
  • Admission applications: accept or reject, and print the list
  • Student notes for praise, complaints and discipline, and class monitors
  • Enrolment certificates, textbooks given and ID cards received

Classes, subjects and timetable

Set up your school once and every module follows it.

  • Add, rename, reorder or hide classes and levels, such as a madrasa level, without losing data
  • Subjects for each class, used in marks, timetable, homework and lesson plans
  • Weekly timetable by class and section, printed on A4 with the principal's signature line
  • Syllabus files and study notes that students download from their panel

Attendance

Daily attendance for students and staff, with nothing to add up by hand.

  • Present, absent, sick, leave and late
  • Attendance by date, and a printed A4 list of the whole class or only those who were away
  • Staff attendance with check-in time and half days, and a monthly register
  • "Mark all present" to save time

Scan station and devices

Save time at the gate: scan an ID card and the person is marked present at once.

  • QR code ID cards read with a USB scanner or the computer's camera
  • Late arrivals marked automatically after the time you set
  • At the end of the day, everyone who did not scan is marked absent
  • Import attendance from fingerprint, face or card devices (CSV, TXT, DAT or Excel)

Exams and results

From marks entry to the parent report card, with the marking rules your school uses.

  • Marks for each exam, class, section and subject
  • Result sheets from the mid-term, final and second-chance exams
  • Passed, conditional and failed results with automatic totals and percentages
  • Parent report card: one A4 page per student with marks, result, attendance and fees still owed
How grading works

Teachers' workspace

Teachers work only with their own classes.

  • Attendance, marks, homework with a due date, and study notes
  • Lesson plans sent for review, then approved or returned with a note
  • Progress notes about each student, for example academic or behaviour
  • Their class's fees, read only

HR and staff

Everyone who works at the school: teachers, office staff, guards, drivers and others.

  • Staff profiles with staff codes, and import from a CSV or Excel template
  • Employee documents such as tazkira and degrees, with expiry dates and a list of what is missing
  • Contracts: permanent, fixed term, part time and probation
  • Leave balances and requests; nobody approves their own leave
  • Evaluations, warnings that teachers confirm they have read, and HR reports

ID cards

Student and staff ID cards printed by the school itself.

  • Four designs, with your colours and logo
  • Nine or ten cards on one A4 page
  • A QR code for scan attendance, holding only the ID and a check code, no private data

Fees and receipts

Every afghani accounted for, with a printed receipt for every payment.

  • Monthly and one-time fee types, given to a whole class in one step
  • Payments into the cash box or bank, with a receipt in two copies on one A4
  • Scholarships as a percentage or a fixed amount
  • Fee card for each student and a month-by-month fee report

Expenses, income and payroll

Clear money records that the owner can trust.

  • Expenses with the bill attached; large expenses wait for the owner's approval
  • Other income, such as donations or canteen rent, and transfers between accounts
  • Payroll from HR records, with absences, unpaid leave and salary advances deducted automatically
  • Cash and bank balances, a daily cash book and an audit log that cannot be changed

Transport and courses

School buses and short courses next to normal classes.

  • Buses with driver, helper, stops and times, and the students on each bus
  • Transport requests from students, with a waiting list and free-seat reports
  • Short courses such as language, computer or Kankor preparation, with fees and attendance

Notice board and more tools

The small daily tasks of a school office, in one place.

  • Notices with attachments for teachers, students and staff
  • One search box for students, teachers, staff, receipt numbers and courses
  • Visitors, staff notes, library and hostel
  • Reports to print or download for Excel

Questions from schools

If your question is not answered here, contact us and we will reply.

1. How do we get started?

We show you a demo and agree on what your school needs. Then we install the system on your school's main computer, activate it for your school, set up your classes and train your staff.

2. Does it need internet?

No. It runs on one main computer in your school, and the other computers open it in the browser over the school network.

3. Can we bring our existing data from Excel?

Yes. Staff can be imported from a CSV or Excel template, and student lists can be exported to Excel. We help you move your existing records when we set up the system.

4. Can students and staff use ID cards for attendance?

Yes. The system prints ID cards with a QR code. At the scan station a USB scanner or the camera reads the card and marks the person present. Attendance can also be imported from fingerprint, face or card devices.

5. Which languages does it support?

Dari, Pashto and English, with right-to-left screens and Solar Hijri dates. Each user can choose their language.

6. What happens if the computer breaks?

Keep your backups on a USB drive. The system can be installed again and the backup restored, so your records come back.

7. Can it be adapted to our school?

Yes. Fees, exam rules, reports, user roles and the modules you use can be adjusted to your school.

8. How much does it cost?

The price depends on the number of students and the modules you choose. Contact us for a quote.
