Entrance Exam Preparation

Digital lessons, practice tests and learning resources that help students prepare for the national university entrance exam.

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Entrance Exam Preparation

The challenge

Students preparing for the entrance exam needed good practice material and a way to test themselves in real exam conditions.

What we built

We built a learning platform with lessons, timed practice tests and results, so students can see their progress and focus on weak subjects.

How it works

  • Learning management systemOrganises lessons by subject and level, and tracks each student’s progress.
  • Content managementThe education team adds and updates lessons and questions without needing a developer.
  • Timed practice testsTests run with a timer, like the real exam, and are scored automatically.

Key features

  • Lessons by subjectOrganised by subject and topic, following the exam syllabus.
  • Timed practice testsReal exam conditions with a timer and automatic scoring.
  • Results and progressScores by subject and over time, so students see where they improve.
  • Study suggestionsWeak topics are shown after every test.
  • Question navigatorJump between questions and mark ones to review.
  • Content managementThe education team adds lessons and questions without a developer.
  • CountdownDays left until the exam, to keep students on track.
  • Mobile friendlyLearn and practise on any phone.

We can also add

Options we can build on top of this system for a new client.

  • Offline mode for low connectivity
  • Video explanations for every answer
  • Leaderboards and challenges
  • Teacher dashboard for class groups
  • AI tutor for difficult questions
